AS FORMS

Privacy policy

Last updated 13 Oct 2026

AS Forms is an expense, mileage and timesheet app made by Aidan Lamb. It is available as an Android app and as this website.

Your data stays on your device

Your claims, receipts, journeys, timesheets and settings are stored on your phone (Android app) or in your browser (web app). There is no AS Forms server. The developer does not collect, receive or see any of your data. There are no analytics, no tracking and no ads.

Optional cloud sync

Google Drive: if you connect it, the app uses only the drive.file permission. It can read and write only the files it creates itself (a backup zip and yearly receipt archives), in a folder called “AS Forms”. It cannot see your other Drive files. It also reads your account email address to show which account is connected.

OneDrive: if you connect it, the app uses only its own app folder (Apps › AS Forms) and reads your account name and email to show which account is connected.

These files go straight between your device and your own Google or Microsoft account. They never pass through the developer.

Postcode and route lookups

To work out mileage, the app sends the postcodes you enter to postcodes.io. It then sends the matching map coordinates to the OSRM routing service to get the road distance. Only postcodes or coordinates are sent, nothing else.

Location

Your location is used only when you tap “Use my location” on a journey. It is used once to find the nearest postcode and is not stored or shared anywhere else.

Receipts and email

Receipt reading (OCR) runs on your device. When you send a claim, the app opens your own mail app with the email ready. It never sends email by itself.

Deleting your data

  • Disconnect Google Drive or OneDrive in Settings › Sync and Backup.
  • Delete the app, or clear its data (Android) or this site’s data (browser).
  • Remove access at any time in your Google account or Microsoft account settings. Files the app saved in your Drive or OneDrive stay there until you delete them.

Google API Services

AS Forms’ use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
