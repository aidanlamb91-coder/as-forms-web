Assumptions and disclaimer

This is a planning estimate, not tax advice. The slider is an English (rUK) estimate of the net cost of the sacrifice. We solve for the matching gross monthly amount, then apply the same monthly PAYE maths as the AS Forms app (2026/27 Scottish bands, Class 1 NI, Plan 4 at 9% over £33,795) for your real cost, treats the year as 12 equal months with a cumulative tax code, and ignores bonuses, overtime, other income, foreign tax and Scottish-rate relief on non-salary-sacrifice pension. The car is assumed electric, so the benefit-in-kind is the list price × 4% and is taxed through your tax code at your top rate; it does not attract employee NI or student loan. Adjusted net income = taxable pay after all salary sacrifice + car benefit. Child Benefit charge and Tax-Free Childcare are worked out on your income only. Lease insurance, maintenance and early-termination terms vary by scheme. Check with your employer and HMRC before signing.

  1. Company car benefit appropriate percentage, 2026/27: 0 g/km = 4%. HMRC, Work out the appropriate percentage (480 Appendix 2), updated 6 April 2026; EIM24705. Cars with CO2 ≤ 75 g/km are outside the optional-remuneration (OpRA) rules, so the BIK, not the sacrificed salary, is taxed.
  2. Scottish income tax 2026/27 bands, NI thresholds (PT £1,048/m, UEL £4,189/m) and Plan 4 threshold £33,795 as used in AS Forms; NLW £12.71/hr from 1 April 2026 and LEL £129/week: HMRC rates and thresholds for employers 2026 to 2027.
  3. England, Wales and Northern Ireland (rUK) income tax 2026/27: personal allowance £12,570 (tapered from £100,000), basic 20% on the first £37,700 of taxable income, higher 40% to £125,140 of income, additional 45% above that (GOV.UK income tax rates). The slider uses this English estimate of the net cost of the sacrifice (tax + NI + student loan, before BIK); your real cost uses Scottish rates on the same gross amount.
  4. Child Benefit 2026/27: £27.05/week eldest, £17.90/week each other child (GOV.UK). High Income Child Benefit Charge: 1% of the benefit per £200 of adjusted net income over £60,000, the full amount at £80,000 (GOV.UK).
  5. Tax-Free Childcare: the government adds £2 for every £8 paid, up to £2,000 per child per year (£4,000 if disabled), children 0–11; not available if you or your partner expects adjusted net income over £100,000 (GOV.UK). Scotland's 1,140 hours of funded early learning and childcare for all 3–4 year olds (and eligible 2 year olds) is not income-tested (mygov.scot); enter only spend beyond those hours.
  6. Statutory maternity, paternity, adoption and shared parental pay use average weekly earnings after salary sacrifice; first 6 weeks of SMP/SAP are 90% of AWE, then the lower of £194.32 or 90%.
